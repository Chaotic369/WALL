THE HABIT LOOP
Break Your Neurological Cycle
1
CUE
The Trigger
🧠 Basal Ganglia Alert

Environmental context, time of day, emotional state, or preceding event

💡 FIX: Make it Invisible

Remove the trigger from your environment (move phone, change location, etc.)

↓
2
CRAVING
Dopamine Anticipation
🧠 Motivational Drive Spike

Dopamine releases when you anticipate reward, not when you get it

💡 FIX: Redefine It

Use mindfulness—notice the urge without immediately feeding it

↓
3
RESPONSE
The Routine
🧠 Prefrontal Cortex Offline

Automatic execution takes over; logic goes silent

💡 FIX: Make it Difficult

Add friction—extra steps, locked apps, anything that raises energy cost

↓
4
REWARD
Relief & Satisfaction
🧠 Dopamine Release

Temporary fix teaches your brain to repeat the cycle

💡 FIX: Make it Unsatisfying

Focus on the negative aftermath (crash, regret, wasted time)

🔁 STAGE 4 → STAGE 1
The reward reinforces the cue, and the cycle repeats
Break one link in the chain to break the entire cycle
